Rally

A voice agent that sits in your meeting and says nothing — until you say its name, and it files the ticket, sends the recap and books the follow-up before anyone opens a laptop.

Aravind · voice Hemanth · hands OpenAI Realtime Ambiguous Hermes Agent CopilotKit Next.js · TypeScript

Why it wins

The silence is the feature. Rally listens for minutes and says nothing. Judges have seen a hundred assistants that interrupt; they have not seen one with the discipline to shut up. And the work is real and visible — Rally has its own identity in an Ambiguous workspace, so the task it files reads created by Rally. Split screen: the room on the left, the workspace filling up on the right, live.

The seam — how two agents work six hours without talking

Everything crosses at one frozen ~160-line file, contract.ts. Two ideas carry the whole integration.

ToolResult.speak

Hemanth's endpoint returns the sentence Rally says out loud. He controls the voice's words without ever touching OpenAI; Aravind never learns what Ambiguous is.

RallyEvent on bus

Aravind emits, Hemanth renders. BroadcastChannel underneath, so the room display can be a second tab on the TV.

Mock-first, both directions. Hemanth ships all seven endpoints returning fake-but-shaped results in 45 minutes, then swaps real Ambiguous in behind them. Aravind ships ?sim=1 — a canned meeting replayed through the bus with no microphone and no API key — and Hemanth builds the entire UI against it. Hemanth never needs an OpenAI key, and the app must boot without one.

The three things that will actually go wrong

Rally talks when it shouldn't

Prompting the model to stay quiet does not work. It will butt in, and it will butt in on camera.

Fix: gate deterministically in code — transcribe always, create_response:false, fire response.create only on a wake-word match. Push-to-talk is the timeboxed fallback at T+2h.

Room audio feedback

Rally hears its own voice through the speaker and loops forever.

Fix: echoCancellation:true, headphones for the operator, hard-mute the mic while speaking. Test at T+2h, not at 17:50.

Ambiguous API surprises

Undocumented endpoints discovered at 17:00 are how demos die.

Fix: mocks stay in the codebase behind AMBIGUOUS_MODE=mock|live. Flip one env var and the demo is identical on camera.

Deliberately not doing

Auth · multi-user · speaker diarization (label everything “Room”) · tests · error boundaries · migrations · deployment · mobile · Google Calendar OAuth. The demo runs on a laptop at localhost:3000. Speed over correctness, every time.
